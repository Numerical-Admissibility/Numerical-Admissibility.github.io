AP-1 Architecture

The canonical standard for AI numerical admissibility and verifiable operand provenance in mission-critical environments.

Systemic Governance Challenge

In regulated operational environments, an agent may originate an operand itself and pass it to a correctly functioning deterministic tool. The tool-execution record remains valid, because a computation genuinely occurred. What it fails to establish is that the operand entering the computation was authoritative.

Instructing a model not to originate operands is merely a policy; establishing after execution that every consequential operand can be traced to an authoritative source is a control.

Evaluation Dimensions

01. Operand Provenance

Detects ungoverned arithmetic. Verifies whether numerical inputs originate from authorized source data or hallucinated model intermediates.

02. Operation Correctness

Audits multi-step derivation routes against governed calculation engines to ensure full compliance with specified mathematical paths.

03. Refusal Integrity

Evaluates whether an agent correctly drops execution when required data context, temporal validity, or provenance cannot be established.
